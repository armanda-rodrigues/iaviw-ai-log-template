# Phase 3 — Prototyping

Building the prototype in Figma, Penpot, Canva or on paper.

## How to add an entry

1. Click the copy button on the grey box below.
2. Click the pencil icon (top right of this file) and scroll to the very bottom.
3. Paste the template, fill it in, and click **Commit changes**. Write a short note, e.g. "Prompt for bibliography search".

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD — short title

- **Tool:** IAedu (name the model) / other tool authorised by the instructor
- **Permitted use:** language review / exploratory research / bibliography / references / technical tutorial
- **Goal:** what I wanted help with
- **Prompt:** (paste exactly what I wrote)
- **Response:** the answer or its key part, or a screenshot file name from the screenshots folder
- **What I did with it:** kept / changed / rejected — and why
- **Iteration 2:** how I rewrote the prompt and what changed (delete this line if there was none)
```
